Steam Market FAQ ↗
How prices and alerts work

Check price requests an EUR quote from Steam’s public, undocumented price overview endpoint. Quotes may be cached for 10 minutes. Steam can limit or stop access. No login, proxy rotation or account cookies are used.

History starts with your own checks, up to 60 observations per item. It is not Steam’s historical chart. Manual observations are labelled. Target notices appear here after a check, with no background polling or closed-app notifications.

Up to 12 items are saved locally. Export a backup before changing browsers or clearing site data. Import replaces this app’s list after confirmation. No analytics, paid API or AI calls.
